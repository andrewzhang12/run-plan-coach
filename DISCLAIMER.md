# Important limitations and responsible use

## Experimental LLM-based software

Run Plan Coach is an experimental, LLM-based planning aid for education and exploration. Its prompts, code, example plans and documentation were developed with AI assistance. It is not a professional training guide, a certified coaching service, or a substitute for assessment by a qualified running coach, clinician, physiotherapist or dietitian.

An LLM can invent facts or sources, misinterpret instructions, miss constraints, miscalculate a session, or produce confident but inappropriate advice. Different models, versions, prompts and conversations can produce different plans. A plausible explanation does not prove that a recommendation is correct. Check consequential details and original sources before relying on them.

## Health and training limits

The tool cannot examine you, diagnose a condition, assess medical readiness, prescribe rehabilitation, or establish safe training intensity or load. It is not intended for emergency assessment or individualized management of injury, illness, pregnancy, medication effects, eating disorders or other clinical circumstances. Seek appropriate professional guidance when those circumstances affect training.

Training carries risk, including injury and excessive fatigue. Do not continue a session simply because a generated plan, device score or validator says it fits. Symptoms, illness, unsafe conditions and qualified professional advice take priority over a schedule. Weight, nutrition and recovery suggestions are not individualized clinical or dietary care; do not use a race or weight goal to justify underfueling or overriding health concerns.

No race result, improvement, injury prevention, recovery timeline or suitability for a particular runner is promised. Human review and adjustment are needed; neither a coach's review nor a software check eliminates all risk.

## What testing establishes

The automated tests check selected programming, scheduling and bookkeeping behavior. A result labelled `structurally_consistent` is not a safety approval, medical clearance, fitness assessment or proof of an effective training plan.

The included runner profiles, feedback and sample plans are explicitly artificial. Agent role-play did not involve real training, clinical testing or race outcomes. It cannot demonstrate that a runner would tolerate a plan or benefit from it. Synthetic examples are not plans for readers to copy without assessment. The project has not been validated in a prospective real-runner study.

## Data and third-party services

The repository does not itself include a live wearable integration or require an account connection. Using its prompts with an LLM service may transmit your inputs to that service under its own data-handling terms. Local Python checks do not make the surrounding chat private, encrypted or confidential.

Share only information you intend that service to receive. Do not commit personal health information, precise routes, credentials, access tokens or identifiable runner records. An ignored folder is a convenience, not a security boundary: it does not encrypt files, remove previously committed data or prevent disclosure through tools or logs. Review staged changes and repository history before publishing. Any future data adapter requires a separate review of permissions, provenance and privacy.
