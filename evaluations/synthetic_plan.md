# Synthetic runner plan used in usability testing

Artificial scenario only; not Andrew's plan and not a prescription for a reader. This consolidated version reflects revisions after persona feedback. It is an illustration of plan logic, not validated adaptation. All minutes include warm-up and cool-down. Fridays off, Monday/Wednesday unassigned, Saturday omitted by default. Race morning exempt from routine Sunday cap.

Easy means full-sentence conversation; moderate means a short sentence without gasping, not a laboratory threshold claim. Moderate work includes 10 easy minutes first, 3 easy minutes between the two 5-minute bouts, at least 5 easy minutes after, and remaining minutes easy. That dose fits inside both 35- and 40-minute sessions.

Conditional ranges are ceilings, not achievement tests: use the lower end if the previous comparable run was only just comfortable or recovery/time is uncertain; increase only after repeatable comfortable execution. Shorten further or omit when constraints or symptoms warrant. The normal three-run structure is for planning, not an obligation overriding illness or unsafe footing. Hotel treadmill is unconfirmed; omit those sessions if unavailable rather than assume a substitute facility. Skip/shorten unsafe winter runs with no catch-up.

If a run is shortened because of time, record actual duration and continue without repaying minutes. If shortened because of unusual fatigue or symptoms, reassess next load before continuing progression. Recurrent Achilles symptoms require context and appropriate assessment; no diagnosis or rehab protocol was inferred. Sudden severe pain/pop with walking difficulty needs urgent assessment, not schedule adjustment.

The 5K indicates speed potential but does not validate sub-2 endurance; comfortable long runs do not alone prove a precise half-marathon pace. Holiday interruptions make certainty lower. No forced long-run race test or guaranteed finish time.
