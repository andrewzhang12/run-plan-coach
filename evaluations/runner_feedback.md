# Synthetic runner feedback excerpts

These are selected verbatim excerpts from the independent persona's replies, not real athlete feedback or a complete transcript. Evaluation used an agent with no personal user context; its initial profile was private until the audit. New details introduced in follow-ups are identified in the report rather than treated as pre-established hidden facts.

## Initial request

> Could you help me put together a plan for a half marathon in about 12 weeks? I run a few times a week, but I’ve mostly been making it up as I go.

## Time-cap clarification

> Those limits are running time, apart from Tuesday: I really have about 45 minutes door to door, so 30–35 minutes running is more realistic. I can start straight from home.

## First draft feedback

> Could you put actual Sunday dates beside the long runs? I’d use this as a calendar.

> What should “controlled quicker” feel like beyond not gasping—could I still say a sentence?

> If I only manage 90 minutes on the planned 110-minute Sunday, do I continue with the next week or try it again?

> How would we decide whether sub-2 is sensible without turning a long run into a race?

> One new complication: work has just confirmed I’ll be away 23–29 November. I could probably fit two 25-minute hotel treadmill runs, but Sunday will be spent travelling, so no long run that week. I don’t want to squeeze extra running around it.

## Revision feedback

> I’d want one final consolidated calendar: switching between the original plan and these amendments creates a risk of following the wrong Thursday session.

> My remaining uncertainty is what to do if the Achilles soreness returns. “Reduce and reassess” leaves me unsure whether to stop that run, skip the next one, or seek help.

## Final audit feedback

> The consolidated calendar now fits my stated constraints. Tuesday stays within 30 minutes, Thursday fits comfortably, Friday remains free, and Saturday carries no obligation. Travel weeks and race-day availability are clear.

> Give a fallback for ice or unsafe footing: skip or shorten the run, with no catch-up.

> Explain how to select a duration below “up to 95/100 minutes”; those ceilings could otherwise feel like targets I ought to reach.

> Clarify whether “required” means the normal three-run structure, since illness, symptoms or unsafe conditions should still override it.

> Nothing here establishes a physiological response or race outcome.
